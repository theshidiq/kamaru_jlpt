# Handoff: a second Read Along passage in Today's Study

From the content session, 2026-10-16. For the software session.

## Why
In `/study` the Watch step plays the Lesson, whose reading part is the Read
Along passage word for word. The Read Along step then shows that same
passage again. Kamal wants that step to show a **different passage on the
same topic**, with the furigana and translation toggles.

## What content has done (committed)
- `src/lib/passages_bjt_alt.ts` exports `PASSAGES_BJT_ALT: SectionPassage[]`.
  It has one passage for each of BJT 1-1 … 3-5, and every new chapter will add five.
  Same type and token format as `PASSAGES_BJT`. All 25 section words are
  tappable, and `targetWords` is filled in.
- Generated by `scripts/build-bjt-passages.py` from `scripts/bjt_passages_alt.py`.
- **Text only: there is no audio and no manifest entry.** Formats can differ
  from the first passage (for example, 1-4 is a memo in both, and 2-2 is a
  notice instead of an email). All formats are ones `FormattedPassage`
  already renders.

## What the app needs
1. **Today's Study, Read step:** when `PASSAGES_BJT_ALT` has the section,
   show it in place of the first passage. A prop on `SectionReaderView`
   would do, e.g. `variant="alt"`, or pass the passage in. Kamal's answer:
   **Today's Study only**. `/read/:chapter/:section` and the Lesson keep the
   first passage. If there is no second passage, fall back to the first
   one, as today.
2. **No audio for it.** It must not look up the manifest under the same id,
   or it would find and play the first passage's audio, whose timings would
   not match. The step is completed with the Next button (`StepFooter`), as
   there is no `onEnded`.
3. **Toggles without audio (Kamal: "they're not showing").** With audio
   they work today: あ/EN sit in the desktop `AudioBar`, and on phones in the
   inline bar plus the floating side buttons. But the floating side buttons
   render only when `audio && controlsHidden` (`SectionReader.tsx`, near the
   `createPortal`), and `AudioBar` only when there is audio. So on a text-only
   passage, あ and EN scroll away with the inline bar on both phone and
   desktop. Keep あ / EN reachable while reading a text-only passage, for
   example the floating pair without the play button, or a sticky bar.
4. Optional: a small label telling the two apart, e.g. "Read Along · 2" /
   "Baca Bersama · 2", through `t()` in both languages.
